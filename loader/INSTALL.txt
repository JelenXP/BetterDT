BetterDT - quality-of-life mods for Devices Tycoon
==================================================

BetterDT adds a few handy buttons to the game (see "What you get" below).
It does not change any game file and it does not touch your saves.


HOW TO INSTALL
--------------
1. Open the game folder:
   Steam > Library > right-click "Devices Tycoon" > Manage > Browse local files.
   A folder opens. You should see "Devices Tycoon.exe" in it.

2. Copy everything from this zip into that folder:
   - BetterDT_x64.ext.dll   (goes right next to "Devices Tycoon.exe")
   - the "mods" folder      (also right next to "Devices Tycoon.exe")
   - BetterDT-INSTALL.txt   (this file)
   Do not put them into a new subfolder like "BetterDT\" - the game would not find them.

3. Start the game as usual from Steam.

4. Check that it works: open the game Settings. On the left there is a new "BetterDT" section.


WHAT YOU GET
------------
- FIND EMPLOYEES: next to every agency there is a second button with a number. It keeps searching
  until your office is full. The AUTO-HIRE checkbox (top right) hires everyone who is found.
- UPGRADE EMPLOYEES: under the game's button (now "UPGRADE DESIGNERS" etc.) an "ALL TO MAX" button trains
  everyone of all professions to the maximum level (as long as you have money and research points).
- RESEARCH LAB: next to the game's button (now "Research tab") "Research all" researches everything
  on all tabs, for research points and money (the button is grey when you cannot afford it).
- Game speed: the speed button in the corner also offers x2 and x3.
- Adjustable autosave: the game's Autosave setting becomes a slider from 5 to 360 game days (default 90).
- Low-end mode for older computers: limit to 30 FPS and turn off blur (Settings -> BetterDT -> Mods, off by default).
- Endless game: keep playing after the end of the game in 2101 ("Continue game" on the final screen).
  Based on a mod by M1lan.
- NEW GAME: "Custom start" under the start years starts the company in any month and year (up to the latest
  year you reached), with research and rivals set between the game's start years.
- NEW GAME: the country cards show tax, research and the country effects, and the countries can be sorted by
  what you need (lowest tax, most money, ...); the best three get a star.
- Country effects: a country with a low cost of production pays lower salaries, more international trade sells more.
- NEWS: an "Auto continue" checkbox next to OK closes news with a single OK by itself after 3 seconds
  (1 to 10 in Settings -> BetterDT -> Mods). Dialogs with a choice are never closed.
- Real company names (game 1.4+): Sony, Intel, Samsung, ... instead of Somy, Infel, Samsong, ...
- INVESTING: buy as many shares as your money allows, up to the game's 49 % limit, in one purchase.
  Based on a mod by M1lan.
- MY STORES: next to the game's "Upgrade all", "All to max" keeps upgrading every store while the money
  lasts (it asks first, with the money it will spend).
- A high fixed FPS no longer slows the game down, and it can go above the game's 165 limit
  (Settings > BetterDT > Mods).
- MY OS UPGRADE: the screen no longer stutters.
- Laptops with two graphics cards: the game runs on the faster one.

Each of these can be turned off in Settings > BetterDT > Mods.

The game's own settings are saved as soon as you change them, without the Apply button.


UPDATES
-------
BetterDT updates itself: when a new version is out, it downloads it when the game starts and
tells you in the main menu. "Restart now" uses it right away, "Keep playing" uses it the next
time you start the game. You can turn this off in Settings > BetterDT.


HOW TO UNINSTALL
----------------
Delete BetterDT_x64.ext.dll and the "mods" folder from the game folder. That's it.


PROBLEMS?
---------
- No "BetterDT" section in the settings: BetterDT_x64.ext.dll is not next to "Devices Tycoon.exe".
- White screen with a small sad face: BetterDT reloads the game by itself (load your last save).
  The main menu then offers "Report problem", which prepares a bug report for you.
- A game update or "Verify integrity of game files" in Steam does not remove BetterDT.
- If something goes wrong, the file mods\betterdt.log says what happened.
  Report problems at https://github.com/JelenXP/BetterDT/issues
  or ask on Discord: https://discord.gg/JdQEMNNTrN
